Interpretation
0x753E4 loads the byte at 0xA802C745, settings base 0xA802C56C + 0x1D9.
0x753EC jumps to 0xBF400 + 0x34C = 0xBF74C. The delay slot executes first.
After SAVE, the incoming selector in %o0 is available as %i0.
0xBF758 stores the selector at 0xA802CB09; 0xBF760 stores zero at 0xA802CB08.
The latter store is in the delay slot of the panel-state check call.
0xBF768 skips the write when that check returns zero.
0xBF788 passes the two-byte buffer pointer in %o5.
0xBF790 selects register 0x072; the write call is at 0xBF7A0.
0xBF7A4 sets bus address 0x60 in the call delay slot.
The subsequent call at 0xBF7CC reads the same register.
The complete setter continues beyond this excerpt with comparison and retry handling.
